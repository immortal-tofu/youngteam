the village

valseigne is a quiet village in the jura. 412 inhabitants. a town hall, an inn, a bakery, a peat bog, and a municipal council of seven.

it is not on any map. it has no insee code. no road sign points to it. and yet — the town hall has a website, and the villagers answer their email.

something happened there. nobody will tell you what.

how it plays

this is an investigation played by email. no app, no account. you read the village's websites, you notice what doesn't add up, and you write to the people who live there. every villager has an address. every villager answers — in their own way.

some will help you. most will lie. ask the right person the right question, and pull the thread.

the villagers write in french. take your time — so do they.

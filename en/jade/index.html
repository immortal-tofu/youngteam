the hunt

seven riddles. a set of coins worth at least €300. free to play, and only one person could claim it.

the riddles are in french. each one carries an english translation underneath, there only to help you follow.

how it ended

the seven riddles were solved.

the solver asked that the win go to the community instead: leads had been traded, finds had been shared, and as they saw it nobody had walked the road alone. the prize was turned down. the chest goes back into play in a future hunt.

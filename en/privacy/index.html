privacy

privacy policy for the discord bot

what the bot sees

the bot only operates on the young team discord server. it can read what discord passes on to it: your discord id and username, your roles on the server, and messages posted in the channels it has access to.

it doesn't read your direct messages, except the ones you send to the bot itself.

what we do with it

we use this data to run the server and the games (announcements, roles, moderation) and for nothing else. we don't sell it, share it or use it for advertising.

the bot doesn't keep a database of players. whatever is stored is stored by discord, under its own privacy policy.

your rights

you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to games@youngteam.net. leave the server and the bot no longer sees anything of yours.

last updated: october 28, 2026. see also the terms of service.
